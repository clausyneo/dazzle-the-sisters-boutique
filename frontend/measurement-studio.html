Before you begin

A calm, comfortable measuring moment

A few small steps help keep your measurements useful and consistent.

01

Use a flexible tape

Use a soft sewing tape that follows your body without stretching. Keep it level and comfortably snug, never tight.

02

Stand naturally

Measure over light clothing, relax your shoulders and breathe normally. Do not pull in your stomach or hold your breath.

03

Ask for a hand

A trusted helper can make shoulder, back and length measurements easier. Recheck the tape position before recording.

04

Keep it private

Please do not upload body photographs or share unnecessary sensitive information. A number and a short fit note are enough.

The beginner's guide

Where does the tape go?

These simple illustrations are a guide. Keep the tape flat against the clothing and avoid twisting it.

Bust / Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of the chest, keeping it level across the back.

Avoid: pulling the tape tight or letting it slope.

Waist

Measure around your natural waist, usually the narrowest part of your torso. Breathe normally.

Avoid: measuring over a bulky waistband.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, with your feet together and tape level.

Avoid: measuring only around the hip bones.

Shoulder

Measure from the outer edge of one shoulder to the other, following the natural shoulder line.

Avoid: measuring around the neck.

Armhole

Loop the tape around the shoulder and under the arm where a sleeve seam would sit.

Avoid: pinching the tape into the underarm.

Bicep

Measure around the fullest part of the upper arm, with the arm resting comfortably by your side.

Avoid: flexing the arm or tightening the tape.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the shoulder point along a slightly bent arm to your preferred sleeve end.

Avoid: measuring with the arm locked straight.

Garment Length

Measure from the garment's shoulder or waist starting point down to the hem you want.

Avoid: changing shoes or posture between checks.

Inseam

Measure from the top of the inner thigh down to the ankle or trouser hem position.

Avoid: pulling the tape upward or measuring over shoes.
